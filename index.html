OnePlan Travel

Drop a TikTok. Get a trip.

The iOS app that turns social videos into real group trips. AI pulls the places out of a TikTok or Instagram link, pins them, builds the itinerary and splits every bill.

The magic

1Drop a linkany TikTok or Instagram travel video
2AI pulls the placessaved as Pins, automatically
3The itinerary builds itselfday by day, ready to go
4Split every billgroup expenses, zero spreadsheets

Why now

Gen-Z plans travel on TikTok, not on TripAdvisor, and nobody owns the moment between watching a place and going there. Splitwise only splits, TripIt only schedules, Google Sheets is… a spreadsheet. OnePlan does the whole trip in one flow, native to the videos people already watch.

How it makes money

Three revenue streams: a weekly subscription that unlocks the full plan Marketplace, AI scan credit packs, and an on-chain transaction fee when TripFund money crosses currencies. The Marketplace is already the strongest magnet in the product, with 1,800 opens in 30 days, and every plan in it is one tap away from becoming a paid trip.
